About Our School

Our Vision & Mission

At Om Public School, we believe in nurturing young minds with knowledge, values and confidence. Our vision is to create a learning environment where every child can discover their potential and build a better tomorrow.

🎯 Our Vision

Global citizens with strong values, critical thinking, and ethical character prepared to lead tomorrow's world.

🌱 Our Mission

Quality education for holistic development through academic rigor, modern STEM facilities, and cultural grounding.

Our History

Established in 2005 under the aegis of Om Education Society, Om Public School has grown into a center of excellence in education in Varanasi, known for its dedicated faculty, modern facilities, 100% CBSE board distinction results, and vibrant student community.

Why Choose Us?

Admission Process

Follow these 4 simple steps to enroll your child at Om Public School for Academic Year 2025-26.

1

Enquiry

Fill out our online enquiry form or visit our campus front desk for an orientation and campus tour.

2

Documentation

Submit required certificates (Birth certificate, previous report cards, Transfer Certificate, and Aadhaar card).

3

Interaction

Informal, friendly student-parent interaction with the school principal and academic coordinator.

4

Confirmation

Receive admission confirmation, student ID allocation, uniform kit details, and academic welcome pack.
