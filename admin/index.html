  • Database: Google Spreadsheet with sheets Notices and Admissions.

Admin Credentials:

Admin credentials are securely stored in your Google Apps Script Script Properties (ADMIN_USERNAME and ADMIN_PASSWORD). They are never exposed in client HTML/JavaScript.
